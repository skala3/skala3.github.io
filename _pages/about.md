<p align="justify">
Hi, I'm Sarthak—a data scientist and machine learning engineer focused on developing intelligent systems that tackle real-world challenges. I'm completing my Master's in Data Science at UC San Diego (expected graduation: December 2025), with expertise in generative AI, machine learning, and end-to-end data science solutions. What drives me is a deep curiosity about using machine learning to create interpretable models that uncover meaningful insights from complex datasets while building intelligent systems designed for scale.
</p>

<p align="justify">
Over five years at American Express, I worked as a Senior Data Scientist, leading high-impact initiatives across marketing and advertising data science, LLM optimization, and natural language processing applications. My recent internship at Hewlett Packard Enterprise gave me an opportunity to dive into AI Agents.
</p>

<p align="justify">
During my time at UC San Diego, I've had the privilege of collaborating with Prof. Biwei Huang on developing agentic frameworks for automated causal discovery and with Prof. Armin Schwartzman on leveraging Graph Neural Networks for predicting intelligence from neuroimaging data. When I'm not debugging models at 2 AM, you'll find me exploring California's beaches and trails, one hike at a time.
</p>
