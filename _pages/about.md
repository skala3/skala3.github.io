<p align="justify">
Hello, I'm Sarthak. I'm a Data Scientist and Machine Learning Engineer currently pursuing my Master's in Data Science at UC San Diego. At UCSD, I got the opportunity to work with Prof. Armin Schwartzman on Graph Neural Networks to predict cognitive intelligence from adolescent brain fMRI data, and with Prof. Biwei Huang to design autonomous AI agent pipelines for causal hypothesis generation. This summer, I took these skills to Juniper Networks, where I built AI agents that read network switch documentation and automatically generate testing code—turning technical docs into executable tests.
</p>

<p align="justify">
Before grad school, I spent 5+ years at American Express turning marketing data into goldmines and teaching machines to actually be useful. I fine-tuned LLMs so marketing teams could chat with their promotional plans (yes, like having a conversation with a spreadsheet, but cooler), built Marketing Mix Models that analyzed $60M+ in ad spend and convinced executives to 2x their budgets, and modernized everything with Bayesian inference to probabilistically quantify uncertainty in decisions. I built a customer complaints segmentation model using Latent Dirichlet Allocation (LDA) and BERT embeddings, slashing response times by 75%, leading to quicker resolution.
Other than that, I worked on several analyses to draw actionable insights, built segmentation models and created A/B tests from user's clickstream and demographic data.
</p>
