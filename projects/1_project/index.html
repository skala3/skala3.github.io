Project Overview

A production-ready reinforcement learning agent that uses a prioritized Double DQN to clear Super Mario Bros World 1-1. The agent combines frame stacking, reward shaping, and stable target networks to balance exploration and convergence.

Training runs ~10k episodes with checkpoints every 100 episodes. Evaluation uses a small amount of test-time exploration (~15% epsilon) to avoid local optima, achieving full level completion in training and consistent clears during evaluation. Everything is scripted for repeatable training, evaluation, and checkpoint loading.
