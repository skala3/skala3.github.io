Super Mario RL Agent

Prioritized Double DQN agent that learns to finish Super Mario Bros World 1-1 with frame stacking, reward shaping, and checkpointed training.

RL / DQN 2024 Super Mario Bros

Project Overview

A production-ready reinforcement learning agent that uses a prioritized Double DQN to clear Super Mario Bros World 1-1. The agent combines frame stacking, reward shaping, and stable target networks to balance exploration and convergence.

Training runs ~10k episodes with checkpoints every 100 episodes. Evaluation uses a small amount of test-time exploration (~15% epsilon) to avoid local optima, achieving full level completion in training and consistent clears during evaluation. Everything is scripted for repeatable training, evaluation, and checkpoint loading.

Technology Stack

RL Techniques
  • Double DQN with target network
  • Prioritized experience replay
  • Frame stacking (84x84 grayscale)
  • Reward shaping for forward progress
Tooling
  • PyTorch RL implementation
  • gym-super-mario-bros environment
  • Checkpointed training & eval scripts
  • Logging for training/eval runs

Key Features

Level Completion

Trains to fully clear World 1-1 with 25 successful completions in training and reliable clears in evaluation.

Stable Training

Double DQN, target networks, and prioritized replay reduce instability and overestimation.

Reproducible Runs

Checkpoints every 100 episodes plus evaluation script with tunable epsilon for inference.

Results & Takeaways

Completion

Training: 25 successful full clears; Evaluation: ~5% completion with epsilon=0.15 to avoid local optima.

Progress

Average progress 48% through the level, best X position hits end-of-level flag (3161/3161).

Exploration

Slow epsilon decay and residual exploration at test time prevent the agent from getting stuck in local policies.

Reproducibility

Scripts for training and evaluation with pinned hyperparameters and checkpoint loading for quick experimentation.
