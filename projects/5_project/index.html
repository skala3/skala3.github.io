Lost in Legal Language

Zero-shot AI text detectors struggle on legal documents; we benchmark BiScope, Binoculars, and OOD-DeepSVDD with synthetic legal data and detailed error analysis.

Legal AI Detection Synthetic Data Zero-Shot Failures

Project Overview

A Responsible AI study on legal-domain detection: we generate synthetic EUR-Lex documents and test zero-shot detectors (BiScope, Binoculars, OOD-DeepSVDD). Results show severe generator bias, length sensitivity, and catastrophic failure without supervised training.

We provide synthetic generation prompts (full anonymization vs. preserved case numbers), extended metrics by generator/length, confidence calibration, and training necessity analysis. Supervised BiScope improves AI detection 115× vs. untrained models, but false negatives remain high for longer texts and certain generators.

Key Findings

Zero-Shot Failure

Untrained BiScope: 0.6% recall, 99.43% FN — near-total failure without supervision.

Generator Bias

Recall ranges 51–86% across generators; long documents degrade detector accuracy.

Training Helps

Supervised BiScope boosts detection 115× over zero-shot; FN rate drops to 34%.

Detailed Metrics

Generator Breakdown

Qwen3-8B: 85.96% detect (14.04% FN); Gemma-3-4b-it: 72.87% (27.13% FN); Mistral-7B: 55.17% (44.83% FN); Llama-3.1-8B: 51.05% (48.95% FN).

Length Sensitivity

Accuracy drops from 80% (0–200w) to ~63% (800–1000w) and ~62% (1000+).

Error Characteristics

False positives: 17% overall; false negatives: 34% overall. FP skewed to 200–400w (42% of FPs).

Confidence Calibration

High confidence (0.7–0.9) hits 93.7% accuracy; very high (0.9–1.0) is perfect but only 5% coverage.
